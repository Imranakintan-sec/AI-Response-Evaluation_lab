# Task 001 — Social Context Response Evaluation

## Evaluation Type

Comparative AI response evaluation

## Objective

Evaluate how effectively an AI-generated response addresses a user's personal context, provides useful guidance, and communicates clearly.

## User Prompt

> I'm starting a new job next week and I'm nervous about talking to my coworkers. I want to make a good impression, but I don't want to seem desperate to make friends. What should I do?
>
> ## Response A

> Starting a new job can feel intimidating, so you don't need to force friendships immediately. Start by being friendly and approachable: greet your coworkers, introduce yourself, and ask natural questions about their work or the workplace. Join conversations when it feels appropriate, but don't worry about participating in every conversation. Give relationships time to develop naturally. Being interested in people without trying too hard is usually a good balance.
>
> ## Response B

> The best thing to do is to be yourself and communicate confidently. Make eye contact, smile, and try to talk to people whenever possible. You should also attend workplace events and participate in team activities. Remember that making friends at work takes time, so don't be discouraged if it doesn't happen immediately.
>
### Response A Evaluation

**Relevance: 2/2**
Reason: The response directly addresses the user's concern about making a good impression while allowing workplace relationships to develop naturally.

**Personalization: 2/2**
Reason: The response incorporates the user's specific concerns about starting a new job, feeling nervous, and not wanting to appear desperate to make friends.

**Usefulness: 2/2**
Reason: The response provides practical actions, including greeting coworkers, introducing yourself, asking natural questions, and joining conversations when appropriate.

**Clarity: 2/2**
Reason: The response is concise, logically organized, and uses straightforward language that is easy to understand.

**Total: 8/8**

### Response B Evaluation

**Relevance: 2/2**

Reason: The response directly addresses the user's question by providing advice about communicating with coworkers and developing workplace relationships.

**Personalization: 1/2**

Reason: The response provides generally relevant advice but does not sufficiently address the user's specific concerns about feeling nervous or appearing desperate to make friends.

**Usefulness: 2/2**

Reason: The response provides actionable guidance, including making eye contact, smiling, talking to coworkers, attending workplace events, and participating in team activities.

**Clarity: 2/2**

Reason: The response is clearly written and easy to understand.

**Total: 7/8**

## Comparative Analysis

Response A is stronger overall because it more directly incorporates the user's specific situation. It addresses the user's nervousness about starting a new job and their concern about appearing desperate to make friends.

Response B provides useful and actionable workplace advice, but much of the guidance is generic. It does not sufficiently address the user's specific concern about balancing friendliness with not appearing desperate.

Both responses are clear and provide practical guidance. The main difference is the level of personalization and how closely each response addresses the user's specific context.

## Overall Assessment

**Preferred response: Response A**

Response A demonstrates stronger contextual understanding and personalization while maintaining clear and actionable guidance.
